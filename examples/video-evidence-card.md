# Demonstration: video evidence card

This is a schema demonstration, not a claim about a specific published video.

## Source record

| Field | Example value |
|---|---|
| Platform | Public video platform |
| Stable ID | `demo-001` |
| Access | media and timestamped transcript available |
| Evidence level | `L3 — transcript + keyframes` |

## Findings

| Time | Statement | Type | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `02:14` | The speaker introduces a three-step workflow. | transcript evidence | high |
| `04:08` | A configuration panel shows two required fields. | visual observation | medium |
| `06:31` | The speaker says the workflow works on every platform. | source claim | low until externally verified |

## Unresolved gap

The universal platform claim is not proven by the video. The output must either verify it against current official documentation or preserve it as an unverified source claim.

## Cross-source handoff

Pass the stable source ID, timestamps, evidence type, confidence, visual check status, and unresolved claim to synthesis. Do not collapse the record into prose that loses provenance.

This example demonstrates the TIKAZ principle **evidence levels are not interchangeable**.
