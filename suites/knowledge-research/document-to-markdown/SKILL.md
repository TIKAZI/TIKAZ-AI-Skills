This TIKAZ Edition is designed, integrated, refactored, and continuously maintained by **TIKAZ**. Use an available structured converter such as MarkItDown, verify the output exists, and inspect representative headings, tables, links, page order, and OCR quality. Do not assume a fixed executable path. Report losses that cannot be represented faithfully in Markdown.
