# Academic Research

This clean-room TIKAZ Edition is designed, integrated, independently refactored, and continuously maintained by **TIKAZ**.

## Inputs and workflow

Accept a research question, date range, disciplines, inclusion and exclusion criteria, source access, and target output. Plan search terms and source types, screen candidates, record exclusions, assess methodology and relevance, extract claims into an evidence ledger, synthesize agreement and disagreement, and run a peer-review-style critique.

## Output contract

Return search scope, inclusion criteria, source ledger, evidence synthesis, contrary findings, methodological limitations, confidence, draft text if requested, and claims that require revalidation. Separate primary evidence, secondary interpretation, inference, and recommendation.

## Validation and fallback

Verify citations against accessible records and keep dates visible. If full text or a primary source is unavailable, label the evidence level and avoid detailed claims that the accessible material cannot support. Never invent citations, datasets, experiments, or results.

## Example and limits

```text
Use academic-research to compare these papers with declared inclusion criteria, methods, contrary evidence, confidence, and a citation-checked synthesis.
```

This Skill does not guarantee systematic-review completeness unless the search protocol, databases, screening, and reproducibility requirements are actually satisfied.
