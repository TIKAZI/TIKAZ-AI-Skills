# Knowledge and Research Suite

Designed, integrated, refactored, and continuously maintained by **TIKAZ**.

Choose a primary workflow:

- `academic-research` for literature and evidence synthesis.
- `personal-knowledge-memory` for user-controlled reusable project memory.
- `product-strategy` for product decisions.
- `solo-business-operations` for cross-functional solo business work.

`document-to-markdown` is an ingestion helper, not a research authority.

Use this suite when the answer must show where it came from, what disagrees, and what remains uncertain.
