# Solo Business Operations

This TIKAZ Edition is designed, integrated, independently refactored, and continuously maintained by **TIKAZ**.

## Inputs and workflow

Accept the business goal, offer, audience, available hours, budget, current assets, obligations, risk tolerance, and review horizon. Identify the bottleneck, choose the smallest operating system that covers product, acquisition, delivery, support, finance, and maintenance, then sequence work by impact and capacity.

## Output contract

Return a prioritized operating plan, weekly capacity allocation, costs, dependencies, risks, metrics, review cadence, and explicit stop or escalation conditions.

## Validation and fallback

Check that the plan fits declared time and budget. Label estimates and legal or financial uncertainty. When specialist advice is required, prepare the questions and evidence for a qualified professional rather than guessing.

## Example and limits

```text
Use solo-business-operations to turn this product goal into a four-week plan that fits twelve hours per week, with costs, metrics, risks, and review points.
```

This Skill is operational planning, not legal, tax, accounting, investment, or employment advice.
