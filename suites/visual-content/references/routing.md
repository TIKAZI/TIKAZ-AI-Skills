# Routing

- Surreal hand-drawn Chinese article illustration → `xiaohei-article-illustrator`.
- Logistics explainers and information cards → `zhuge-logistics-illustrator`.
- Generic, padded, repetitive copy → `concise-writing-editor` before visual planning.
- Public-domain, Creative Commons, artist-free, or name-your-price music → `legal-free-music`.

Do not blend both illustration identities in one asset.

Examples:
- Example: “Illustrate this Chinese essay with one clear hand-drawn metaphor and alt text.”
- Example: “Explain this warehouse process as a sourced 16:9 logistics card.”
- Example: “Remove filler from this caption, then find lawful music with attribution evidence.”
